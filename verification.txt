2026-10-08 camera latency and FOV controls

Up / Down changes the aim FOV by +/-5 pixels per physical press (1-800).
No repeat timer, focus query or new Windows import. Opposite keys cancel;
aim-key binding reserves both keys. The game camera FOV is unchanged.

The previous renderer reused screen coordinates computed during the entity
scan. A camera turn could therefore leave ESP aligned with an older view.
The update scan also called native ProjectWorldLocationToScreen for every
actor and bone, while the camera could change between those calls.

Now targeting uses one camera matrix per scan. At draw time, cached world
positions/bones are projected with a fresh camera cache sample after GPU
readiness and snapshot exchange. Two matching guarded camera copies reject
a partially updated POV; bad values use the published camera. Invalid
world/controller/pawn/camera links suppress the stale scene. No native game
call, bone lookup, entity scan or new allocation is introduced in drawing.
This does not extrapolate entity movement or add ESP smoothing.

The raw camera layout and projection convention match the supplied spot
GetCameraPOV/WorldToScreen code: CameraCachePrivate 0x1560 + POV 0x10.
Scope zoom is already included in that FOV; it is not multiplied again.

The old presentation interval 1 enabled VSync despite its comment. Replaced
with D3DPRESENT_INTERVAL_IMMEDIATE and SetMaximumFrameLatency(1), preserving
both shared surfaces, their completion queries, and nonblocking submission.
Desktop composition and the separate game renderer can still add latency.
