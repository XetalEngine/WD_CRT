Magic minimum distance for combined Silent + Magic

Aim > Projectiles now shows Magic min dist (m) when both Silent aim and Magic bullet are enabled. Range is 0-1000 meters; default is 0 to preserve current behavior. Below the threshold, Silent redirects each eligible round once with its existing prediction and visibility rules. At or above the threshold, Magic continuously steers to the current selected bone with its own visibility-bypass setting. Magic-only and Silent-only ignore this combined-mode threshold. A Silent round can enter Magic steering when the target crosses the threshold. Returning below it stops continuous steering without redirecting an already-handled round a second time. Magic's dead-target delay applies only at Magic distances.

Uses the existing cached target distance and settings snapshot on the update thread. No new actor scans, distance calculations, native game calls, timers, hooks, threads or imports. The projectile steering math and ownership/flight guards are unchanged. Tests' synthetic key and projectile-subsystem setup helpers compile only under WD_TEST and are absent from the DLL. Diagnostic messages retain the WD_xetal: filter and remain bounded per aim-key hold.
